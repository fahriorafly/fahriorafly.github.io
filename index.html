🐯 I STILL LOVE YOU 🦁

You are still important to me, with or without conversation. You are always in my mind & heart.

(We just stopped talking. But I never stopped thinking about you.)

Thank you for everything. For the love, the laughter, the memories, and all the little moments that made my world brighter. I will always be grateful for the time we shared and the person you were to me.

I’m sorry for the mistakes, the words unspoken, and the times I failed you, deeply.
My heart never meant to hurt yours. I hope our story continues endlessly.

~ Dudut Culukubuy ~
